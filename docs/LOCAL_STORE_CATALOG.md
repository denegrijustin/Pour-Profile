# Local store photo catalog

Discover defaults to confirmed expressions seen in local store photos. Broader research remains available for bottle lookup and existing collections.

Evidence lives in `data/local-store-sightings.json`. Add a batch for each upload with its actual upload date, coverage, and canonical catalog IDs linked to photo numbers. This supports every spirit and wine category. Keep previous batches: not appearing in a partial survey does not mean out of stock. Photo 1 in the initial upload is a home bar and is excluded from store evidence.

For Friday uploads, identify the exact expressions, keep unclear labels pending, research confirmed new expressions across producer and independent sources, then add the batch and rebuild. Do not invent vintage, batch, proof, or reviews from an unreadable label. Wine vintages and different spirit expressions need separate IDs when distinguishable. Upload date is not a capture date or a live stock guarantee.

The first batch includes 24 researched bourbon and whiskey expressions. Other spirits and wine will join the local catalog as their exact labels are confirmed and researched. Uploading photos in chat currently requires this review/build workflow; the app does not automatically ingest a weekly store inventory.
